Privacy Policy

All People Powered  |  Last updated: February 2026

All People Powered ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information you provide to us, including in connection with our SMS outreach program.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Your name and startup or project name
  • Your mobile phone number and/or email address, submitted voluntarily at our events or through our website

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Send you SMS and/or email communications about All People Powered events, programs, and opportunities, including our accelerator and pitch contest
  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Improve our events and community programs

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. SMS Messaging

By providing your mobile phone number to us, you consent to receive SMS messages from All People Powered. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

To opt out of SMS messages at any time, reply STOP to any message we send you. To request help, reply HELP or contact us at holler@rhymecombinator.com.

4. Service Providers

We use Twilio to deliver SMS messages on our behalf. Twilio may process your phone number solely for message delivery purposes. Twilio's privacy policy is available at twilio.com/legal/privacy.

5. Data Retention

We retain your contact information for as long as necessary to communicate with you about our programs, or until you request removal.

6. Your Rights

You may request that we delete or correct your personal information at any time by contacting us at holler@rhymecombinator.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
